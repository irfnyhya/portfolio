    Questions

    Can I exchange a size?

    Yes, within 7 days of delivery, as long as the tags are on and the other size is in stock.

    When will my order ship?

    Orders paid before 3pm ship the same working day.

    How do I pay?

    At checkout: FPX online banking, credit or debit card, DuitNow QR, Touch 'n Go eWallet, GrabPay, ShopeePay, or Atome for 3 interest free payments.

    Do you ship to Sabah and Sarawak?

    Yes, RM15 per order, usually 4 to 7 working days.

    Do the other colours look like the photo?

    Photos show one colour. Ask us in the chat for real photos of the others.

    Can I collect in person?

    Yes, at our studio in Shah Alam, Monday to Saturday, 10am to 6pm.
