Chez Nous

No wall between us

Chez Nous means "at our place". The kitchen is the room: four chefs cook a French menu at the grill, the stove, the pass and the pastry marble, an arm's length from your glass.

Every seat looks at a different station, so we let you choose it. Sit by the charcoal grill if you like fire, at the pass to watch every plate finished, or by the pastry marble for the soufflé.

    Your evening

    Two and a half hours at the counter, in four parts.

    Arrive

    Arrive ten minutes early. Your name card is on your seat. Everyone starts together.

    Watch

    Watch the fire and the pans. Ask anything: the chefs talk you through each course.

    Taste

    Seven courses in two and a half hours. Each plate goes from the pass straight to you.

    Linger

    Stay for coffee and cheese. The second seating has the counter until midnight.

    Before you come

    • Seatings at 6:00 PM and 8:45 PM, Wednesday to Sunday
    • Jalan Telawi, Bangsar (demo address)
    • Park at Bangsar Village, three minutes on foot
    Can we sit together as a group?

    Up to six sit side by side at the counter. For four to six who want to face each other, book the chef's table beside the pastry station.

    Can we take the whole counter?

    Yes. Choose seven or more guests and the request is for the whole room: twelve counter seats and the chef's table, eighteen in all. We confirm by phone within a day.

    What if my seat is taken?

    Taken seats are crossed out for that seating. Try the other seating, another night, or a seat at the same station.

    Can the kitchen cook around allergies?

    Yes, and around most diets. Write it in the notes when you book so the chefs can plan your courses.

    How do I change or cancel?

    Open "My booking" at the top of the page. Cancel up to 48 hours before at no charge.
