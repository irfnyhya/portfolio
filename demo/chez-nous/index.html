ii.About Chez Nous

No wall between us.

Chez Nous is a French kitchen wrapped in a U-shaped counter of black stone, with two seats on each arm and six along the front. The counter is Nero Marquina marble edged in brass, the pass is Calacatta, and a brass chandelier of opal globes hangs over the front. Fluted walnut walls, backlit onyx and a glass-fronted wine wall frame the room. The room stays dark: a gold lamp at every pair of seats, dome lights over the stoves, and a frame of white light around the kitchen. All you see is the food being made.

Every seat looks at something different, so we let you choose yours. Sit by the grill if you like fire, in front of the plating table to watch every plate finished, or by the pastry marble for the soufflé.

vii.FAQ

Before you arrive.

Can we sit together as a group?

Up to six sit side by side. Seats run from the left arm round the front to the right arm, so a group can also sit around a corner and face each other.

Can we take the whole counter?

Yes. Choose seven to ten guests and the request is for all ten seats. We confirm by phone within a day.

What if my seat is taken?

Taken seats are crossed out for that seating. Try the other seating, another night, or a seat at the same station.

Can the kitchen cook around allergies?

Yes, and around most diets. Write it in the notes when you book so the chefs can plan your courses.

How do I change or cancel?

Open "My booking" in the menu. Cancel up to 48 hours before at no charge.
