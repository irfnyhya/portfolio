ii.About Lindap

No back of house, only the pass.

Lindap is a small restaurant built around one long counter. Every seat looks straight into the kitchen, so dinner comes with a view of the fire, the knives and the plating.

There is no set menu. You order what you like, when you like, and the chef talks you through each plate as it lands. With only twelve seats a night, the kitchen cooks for the people in front of it.

See you at the counter.

  • I

    Open

    Nothing happens behind a door. Every step of every dish is cooked within arm's reach.

  • II

    Direct

    Order from the person cooking your food, and ask anything about it.

  • III

    Seasonal

    The menu follows the market and is rewritten each season.

iv.The Evening

Front row to the fire.

Two seatings a night at one long counter. Most evenings run about two hours, so plan to stay.

  1. I

    Take your seat

    Everyone sits at the counter, a few steps from the stove, with a house drink to start.

  2. II

    Meet the chef

    The chef takes your order across the counter and tells you what is best tonight.

  3. III

    Watch it happen

    Each plate is finished in front of you and served the moment it is ready.

vi.FAQ

Before you arrive.

How long is dinner?

About two hours. Each seating starts together, so please arrive on time.

Can I choose where I sit?

Every seat faces the kitchen. Pick a preference when you book and we will do our best.

Can you cater to dietary needs?

Yes, most allergies and preferences. Tell us when you book so the kitchen can prepare.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual. You sit close to the stove, so leave the heavy jacket at home.

Can we book the whole counter?

Yes. Choose twelve guests in the booking, or send us a WhatsApp with your date and we will reply with options.
