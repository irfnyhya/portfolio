ii.Tentang Lindap

Food from home, cooked slowly.

Lindap means shaded: the cool spot under a tree where everyone ends up after lunch. We built the restaurant around that feeling. Unhurried, generous, and a little bit smoky.

The kitchen cooks the dishes we grew up with over charcoal and coconut husk, then plates them to pass around the table. Bring people you like. Order more than you think.

vi.Soalan lazim

Good to know.

Do I need to book?

Not always. Walk ins are welcome when there is space, but weekend evenings fill up, so a booking is the safer bet.

Can you handle allergies?

Yes. Tell us in the booking notes and the kitchen will plan ahead. Many dishes use nuts, shellfish and belacan, so please be specific.

Is it good for kids?

Very. Shared plates suit families, and we have high chairs. Let us know how many you need.

Where do I park?

There is street parking nearby and a paid car park a short walk away. Directions come with your booking confirmation.

Do you do private events?

The whole room can be booked for groups. Send us a WhatsApp with your date and headcount and we will reply with options.
